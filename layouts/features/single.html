From a phishing report to a shared alert

  1. Collect the clues

    Save the report, suspicious domains, and file fingerprints (hashes) together in an event, MISP’s record of an incident or investigation.

  2. Connect the evidence

    See whether a domain or file has appeared in earlier reports. Add notes so teammates can follow what you found.

  3. Share with the right people

    Keep the event inside your organisation or share it with trusted partners. Set narrower sharing rules for individual details when needed.
