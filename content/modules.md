# Extend your intelligence with MISP modules

Use [misp-modules](https://github.com/MISP/misp-modules) to enrich indicators, import data, export intelligence, connect your workflows, and gather data into MISP feeds. Search by service, module name, input type, or requirement.

This catalogue includes the modules in the upstream source. Availability in your instance depends on installed dependencies, service credentials, and configuration. Logos come from the upstream project; modules without a logo use initials.

{{< module-catalog >}}

## Use modules with MISP

Follow the [installation guide](https://misp.github.io/misp-modules/install/) to run the module service and connect it to MISP. Each module's details and source describe its inputs, outputs, and requirements. Module types are expansion (including enrichment and hover lookups), import, export, workflow action, and gathering.
